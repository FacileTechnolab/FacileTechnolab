### ⚡ What We Do Best

- **.NET & Azure Product Engineering:** Designing and building cloud-native products on ASP.NET Core, Blazor, and Azure, tuned for long-term roadmaps.
- **Multi-Tenant SaaS Platform Development:** Turning internal tools or legacy products into secure, multi-tenant SaaS with tenant isolation, billing, and self-service built in.
- **Legacy Modernization to Microsoft Cloud:** Assessing and modernizing legacy .NET, Classic ASP, PHP, or monolith systems into modular .NET 8 and Azure solutions — without losing the business logic that runs the company.
- **AI Development & Integration:** Adding predictive analytics, computer vision, custom ML models, and AI agents (built on Microsoft Agent Framework) to new and existing .NET/Azure applications.
- **Dedicated Microsoft Product Squads:** Small, senior-heavy teams that extend an in-house team's sprint cadence, or staff augmentation to add senior .NET/Azure developers directly into an existing pipeline.
- **Enterprise Application Development:** Ground-up builds on ASP.NET Core and Azure, built for the scale and compliance regulated businesses already operate under.
